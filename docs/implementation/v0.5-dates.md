# v0.5 implementation write-up — dates

Requested by the genodex record entry: record columns hold dates in
pre-reform Russian («21 генваря», «3 сент.», «21.01») and the host needs
numbers. Builds on `textnorm` (v0.1); the analyzer and `ner` are unchanged,
no dependency changed, no `textnorm` table changed, `analyzerVersion` and
`ner.extractorVersion` are not bumped.

## What was built

- `dates.Month(word) (int, bool)` (`dates/month.go`): month number from a
  month word — nominative and genitive, abbreviations with or without a
  dot, pre-reform spelling, any letter case.
- `dates.Parse(text, ctxYear) (Date, bool)` and `Date{Year, Month, Day,
  YearFromContext}` (`dates/date.go`): word dates («21 генваря 1883 года»,
  «21-го января») and numeric dates (`.`, `/`, `-`).
- Scenario 26 (EN + RU), `library.md` section `dates`, godoc examples
  `ExampleParse` and `ExampleMonth`, example program `examples/dates`.

## Decisions

- **Spelling through `textnorm.PreReform`.** The same rules as the analyzer:
  ѣ, і, ѳ and case are folded before the lookup, so the month
  table is keyed by modern spelling (the nominatives with a final hard sign,
  «мартъ» and «августъ», are listed too).
- **The year from the text wins over the context year**; `YearFromContext`
  marks a substituted year. `ctxYear` 0 means no context.
- **Two-digit years are rejected** as ambiguous; years are 1000..9999.
- **Numeric dates are always day.month[.year].**
- **The «г.»/«года» suffix needs a year** («21.01 г.» is not a date).
- **Only attested abbreviations** are in the table (the fix after the
  first draft dropped invented ones).
- **No Julian/Gregorian conversion.** A date is taken as written; the host
  knows the record's calendar.

## Known limits

- Date ranges, «в тот же день», weekdays and church-calendar days are not
  parsed.
- A date inside running text is not found: `Parse` takes the whole string;
  locating dates in a document is up to the host or a future `ner` rule.
