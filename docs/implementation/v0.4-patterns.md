# v0.4 implementation write-up — rule patterns, persons, facts

Consolidates the design ([specs/2026-10-04-v0.4-patterns-design.md](../specs/2026-10-04-v0.4-patterns-design.md))
and the plan ([plans/2026-10-04-v0.4-patterns.md](../plans/2026-10-04-v0.4-patterns.md)).
Builds on [v0.2](v0.2-ner.md) (`rules`, `ner`, `nertest`) and
[v0.3](v0.3-inflect.md). Implemented, not yet released. No dependency
changed; `go.mod` and `go.sum` are untouched.

## What was built

- `rules`: the pattern model (`Pattern`, `Element`, `Repeat`, `Action` with
  `Relabel`/`Boost`/`Label`/`Emit`, `RuleSet.Patterns`, `Active.Patterns`),
  YAML loading with line numbers, compilation to a `Program` (conditions
  `type`/`lemma`/`grammeme`/`token`/`shape`/`any`, `not`, groups, repeats,
  role and action validation, errors `<file>:<line>: <set>/pattern "<name>": …`)
  and a backtracking matcher over a `Lattice` of terms and candidate spans.
- `ner`: patterns run per sentence after triggers, before the
  `RequiresContext` filter; `relabel` retypes with the old reading kept in
  `Alternatives`, `boost` adds evidence, `label` creates spans (a labelled
  span can hold its parts, also on its own whole range, for `Config.Nesting`
  pairs; its `Normal` is composed from the parts); facts (`Result.Facts`,
  `Fact{Kind, Args, Rule}`) are bound to resolved output spans, dropped
  when an argument is missing and de-duplicated; a span ending in a dotted
  abbreviation includes the dot; `extractorVersion` is `ner-3`.
- `nertest/testdata/persons.*`: person rule set, TSV gazetteer and 23 golden
  cases in modern and pre-reform spelling (`TestPersonsGolden`).
- `lexicon extract` prints facts (table rows, JSONL lines); the CLI golden
  test runs the person set with `--ortho prereform`.
- Scenarios 23–25 (EN + RU), reference in `library.md`/`cli.md`, example
  `examples/persons`.

## Decisions

- **P12.** `label` creates spans, `relabel` only retypes; a created span is a
  `Candidate` unless it covers spans the pattern consumed; compile errors
  for the wrong role kind.
- **P13.** Composite spans: nesting only for `Config.Nesting` pairs; a
  labelled span may hold an allowed span on its own whole range; output
  order puts the labelled span before its same-range part; `emit` arguments
  are spans (labelled roles or `type: person` elements).
- **P14.** Element conditions: `shape`, several conditions on one element,
  `not`.
- **P15.** Patterns apply in book order, each on what the previous left;
  one person per match.
- **P16.** A span whose last word is a dotted abbreviation ends after the
  dot; ⚠ re-extract (`ner-3`).

## Deviations from the plan

- Task 6 Step 4 (weight of a labelled span growing with covered parts) was
  not needed and was not applied.
- P16 got one more rule, found by the Task 9 review (commit 0497546): a span
  also takes the dot when a span nested directly inside it and ending at its
  last word takes it, so a nested span never ends past its outer span.
- The CLI test for the person golden set needed `--ortho prereform`
  (`lexicon extract`/`golden` default to `modern`).
- The example's output carried the flag `predicted` on persons whose words
  the tiny base does not know; the final review reversed that rule (below),
  and only the part a pattern labelled over an unknown word keeps the flag.

## Final review

A whole-branch review (2026-10-04) found six interaction defects between
pipeline stages; all are fixed on the branch, each with a test.

- **F1.** A `RequiresContext` part consumed by a `label` was dropped by
  `filterContext`. Now every candidate the match consumed inside the
  labelled range gets context («Иван Мороз» keeps its surname part).
- **F2.** A labelled part could not nest on the range of a labelled outer
  span. Now a same-range inner may be labelled when it was created earlier
  than the outer («ответчик Лаптев»: `person` over a labelled `surname`).
- **F3.** `relabel` and `label` scanned all candidates per match, so
  `Extract` was quadratic in the document size with patterns. They now look
  up a per-start-position index kept by the pattern stage. An unbounded
  repeat stays quadratic in the sentence length; documented, no code change.
- **F4.** A labelled span with nested parts took `Ambiguous` (ambiguous
  abbreviation) and `Predicted` from words its parts had already settled.
  It now takes them only from words outside its parts; `Abbrev` is unchanged.
- **F5.** A third reading of a range shared by a labelled span and its part
  tied against the labelled span. Alternatives and the tie flag now go to
  the part.
- **F6.** A pending fact was dropped when a later `relabel` replaced its
  argument with the existing candidate of the target type. Facts now follow
  the replacement.

Reversed decision: `Predicted` on composite spans (was open question
Q-v04-1, ruled "repeat the flags of every word" during execution). The
final rule is F4; the design (P12), scenario 24, the `persons` example and
the CLI sample follow it.

## Known gaps

- An out-of-range `Repeat` value marshals to a string that cannot be loaded.
- `Program.Actions()` returns the internal slice (documented read-only).
- A weight of 0 means the default 1; negative weights are not rejected.
- No matcher test for a consumed span that dead-ends before another path succeeds.
- `composeNormal` is not recursive: a labelled part nested in a labelled
  span contributes its lemma, not a form composed from its own parts.
- The fixture rule `given-as-patronymic` matches any given + given + surname
  sequence («Иван Анна Сидоров» → «Анна» as patronymic): rule data a host
  should tighten.
- The ambiguity penalty applies to labelled spans by the number of their
  lemma combinations.
- No `ctx` check inside the pattern stage (only between stages).
- An unbounded repeat makes the matcher quadratic in the sentence length
  (documented in scenario 23 and `library.md`).

## Open questions

- **Q-v03-2. Lazy repetitions.** Repetitions are greedy; `*?` is not supported.
- **Q-v03-4. Facts in golden sets.** `nertest` scores spans only.
- **Q-v03-8. Repeated role captures.** A repeated `role` keeps its last repetition.
- **Q-v03-10. Agreed normal forms** (`Span.Normal` for spans without
  dictionary hits through `Registry.Inflect`): needs the head-word/agreement
  rule; ⚠ re-extract. See `docs/todo.md`.
